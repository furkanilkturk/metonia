# Privacy and Boundaries

This public template contains fictional examples only. A real vault may contain
company context, but it should be private and access-controlled.

## Never store

- credentials, tokens, private keys, recovery codes, or secrets;
- regulated personal data, customer exports, or unnecessary identifiers;
- incident details that need a restricted system of record;
- a copied source you are not authorized to retain or share.

## Store carefully

- Company pages should describe operating patterns, not personal performance
  judgments or confidential negotiations.
- Project pages should link to an authorized source of truth rather than clone
  large ticket queues or codebases.
- Learning material must preserve provenance and access constraints.
- Video research must distinguish public observation from confidential strategy.

When uncertain, keep a short pointer to the authorized system and ask the
responsible owner before recording the detail here.

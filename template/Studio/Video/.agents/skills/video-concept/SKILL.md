# Video Concept

Read the Studio/Video `AGENTS.md`, the requested brief, and only the research
needed for this concept. Preserve the creator's voice instead of defaulting to a
generic content template.

Produce a concept that makes these decisions explicit:

- target viewer and the change promised to them;
- one-sentence thesis and differentiated angle;
- opening hook, tension, and payoff;
- ordered story beats with evidence or demonstration needs;
- visual language, on-screen proof, and pacing intent;
- scope boundaries, risks, and the next production artifact.

Generate alternatives only when they expose a meaningful tradeoff, then recommend
one with reasons. Flag unsupported claims for research instead of filling gaps.
Check nearby concepts before creating a new file and link genuine relationships.
Do not expand into a full script or publish anything unless asked.

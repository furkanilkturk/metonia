# Video space

Own video research, concept development, and review. Read [[Video]] before
starting. Use the local `video-research`, `video-concept`, or `video-review`
pack only when it is installed under `.agents/skills/`.

## Boundaries

- `Research/` holds attributable observations and source links.
- `Concepts/` holds briefs, angles, scripts, and creative choices.
- `Inbox/` holds unprocessed material; `Outputs/` holds disposable exports.
- Distinguish fact, inference, and creative proposal.
- Do not put confidential company strategy, unlicensed source material, or
  personal data here. Link to an authorized source instead.

Preview a concept direction before expanding it into a script or production
package. Keep scope, audience, format, and success signal explicit.

# Video Review

Read the governing concept and the artifact under review. Evaluate the work on
its own stated audience, thesis, voice, and constraints.

Report:

1. what already serves the promise and should be preserved;
2. blocking issues in accuracy, structure, comprehension, rights, or production;
3. high-value improvements to hook, evidence, pacing, visuals, and payoff;
4. optional polish, clearly separated from required fixes;
5. a short verification checklist for the next cut.

Anchor feedback to exact sections, timestamps, frames, or asset paths. Separate
objective defects from taste. Verify factual claims against cited/current sources
when accuracy is part of the request. Preserve the creator's voice and do not
silently modify or publish files without authorization.

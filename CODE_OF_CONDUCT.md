# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We commit to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless
of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Use respectful language, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, respect
privacy, and focus discussion on the work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, publication of private
information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub contact.
Reports will be handled promptly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers
may clarify expectations, warn, remove content, or restrict participation.

This short policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.

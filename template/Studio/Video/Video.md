# Video

Video is a focused creative agent space. It intentionally differs from
Learning: learning extracts durable understanding from a video; this space uses
research to develop or evaluate a video idea.

## Areas

- `Inbox/` — new, authorized material to triage.
- `Research/` — source-backed audience, format, and reference observations.
- `Concepts/` — briefs, angles, outlines, and scripts.
- `Outputs/` — exports, review packages, and other disposable artifacts.

Start a concept from [[Concept Template]] and link only the research it needs.

## Local packs

When vendored, this space contains `video-research`, `video-concept`, and
`video-review`. They remain here so an ordinary coding or learning agent never
receives their full instructions.

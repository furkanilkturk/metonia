# Example Company

> Fictional example only. Replace this folder in a private vault; do not treat
> it as a source of real company policy.

## Purpose, customers, and business model

- Mission: help small teams keep shared notes understandable.
- Customers: collaborative knowledge workers.
- Value and revenue: a fictional subscription product with a privacy-first
  positioning.
- Constraint: clarity and reliability take precedence over feature volume.

## Operating model

- Product owns outcome definition; engineering owns technical execution;
  customer support supplies recurring user evidence.
- Weekly planning ranks work by customer impact, risk, and reversibility.
- The directly responsible owner decides reversible changes. Product and
  engineering jointly approve difficult-to-reverse product or architecture work.
- Escalate customer-impacting incidents to the on-call owner and product lead.

## Domain vocabulary

| Term | Meaning | Source or owner |
|---|---|---|
| workspace | A customer's shared knowledge area. | Product |
| note lineage | The evidence and decisions behind a note. | Engineering |

## Engineering and delivery

- Each service has a named owner and a canonical repository remote in its
  project page.
- Changes receive peer review, proportionate automated tests, and a rollback
  plan when customer data or availability is affected.
- Releases are small, observable, and announced to support when behavior changes.

## Security, privacy, and compliance

- Customer content is private by default; never place it in this template.
- Access follows least privilege; credential and incident systems remain the
  source of truth.
- Product changes that alter data handling require product and security review.

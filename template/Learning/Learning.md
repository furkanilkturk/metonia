# Learning

Learning turns authorized evidence into reusable understanding without confusing
the two. Raw material stays evidence; lessons and concepts are maintained
interpretations.

## Areas

- [[Rules]] — evidence and synthesis conventions.
- [[Courses/Index]] — structured learning paths and lessons.
- [[Concepts/Concept Map]] — durable ideas shared across courses.
- [[Recall/Index]] — prompts that test understanding.
- [[Open Questions/Index]] — unknowns worth revisiting.
- `Raw/` — Inbox, Sources, and Assets.
- `Outputs/` — disposable reports and exports.

## Local packs

When vendored, `doc-learning` and `video-learning` are available only under
`.agents/skills/`. Their role is ingestion and synthesis; they are not available
to Work or Studio/Video agents.

# Video Research

Follow the Studio/Video `AGENTS.md` and work only in that agent space. Start from
the stated audience, topic, and intended outcome; ask only for missing choices
that would materially change the research.

Build a research brief that contains:

- the audience's likely question or tension;
- source-backed claims with dates and direct links;
- counterpoints, uncertainty, and claims that should not be made;
- concrete examples or demonstrations worth testing;
- reusable findings linked to existing research notes.

For facts that may have changed, verify them against current primary sources.
Prefer source summaries to long quotations, and obey source licenses and access
limits. Keep raw captures separate from the synthesis. Do not create a concept or
script unless the user asks; finish with the strongest supported opportunities
and open research gaps.
